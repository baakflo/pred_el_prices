2026 came in calmer than the trailing year the recalibration learns from, so its 80 % band runs a little wide there (84 %).

Backtest 2020-01-01 → 2026-09-21, weekly refits, each trained only on days up to two days before its week. Recalibration from 2021 on, fitted on the trailing 365 days. G2 = LEAR with a gradient-boosted correction and neighbour residual load, the best non-neural model.
Inputs: ENTSO-E day-ahead forecasts (DE and 9 neighbours), EPEX prices, TTF gas and EUA carbon settlements (lagged two days). Full record: docs/pred_el_prices_project_plan.md, addendum 2026-09-23.
