Scorecard

Old against new

Old = the tuned quantile network as on the architecture page: 4 networks. New = 12 quantile-head + 12 Johnson's-SU networks, averaged, plus the quarter-hour shape model. Both are recalibrated the same way and trained on identical data each week.

What the 60 days say

Both networks are far ahead of what the site shows today. The live LEAR forecast missed by 28 €/MWh on median over its days; either network by about 17.

Old and new tie hourly. The long backtest found more networks worth about 1 % pinball. Sixty days can't resolve a gain that small; it drowns in day-to-day noise.

The 15-minute layer is the clear win. Predicting each quarter-hour's deviation from its hour cuts 15-minute pinball by about 15 %, with inputs production has, and the fans stay calibrated.

The biggest remaining gap is the wind/solar forecast. Feeding the grid operators' own forecast (published after the auction, so impossible live) would be 8 % better. ICON's hourly solar beat ECMWF's in the 53-day check, so that is the next lever.
